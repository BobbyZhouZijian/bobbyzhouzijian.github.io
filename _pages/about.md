👋 Nice to meet you! My name is **Zijian (Bobby)**. I am a PhD candidate advised by [Bryan Kian Hsiang Low](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~lowkh/research.html) at the National University of Singapore (NUS). Previously, I interned at MiniMax (Beijing), working on large language models, and was a research engineer at the Singapore-MIT Alliance for Research and Technology Centre (SMART) from 2023 to 2025, advised by [Daniela Rus](https://danielarus.csail.mit.edu/) at MIT. Prior to my PhD, I completed my undergraduate studies at NUS, majoring in Computer Science and Mathematics, and interned at TikTok (Singapore) as an ML engineer on the advertisement moderation team.

My research began with a **game-theoretic perspective** on machine learning: how to **value, curate, and attribute data** to make learning more reliable, fair, and efficient. I now study how **language agents learn from experience and improve over time**, through reinforcement learning, adaptive environments, memory, and collaboration.

My current research focuses on:

- **Self-improving agents** — self-play in adaptive executable environments ([SPADE](https://spade-rl.github.io/)), memory and reasoning for long-horizon tasks ([MEM1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15841)), and multi-agent evolution for open-ended discovery ([CORAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01658)).
- **Data-centric learning and prompt optimization** — valuing and attributing data, and selecting in-context demonstrations to make learning more effective and interpretable.
- **Efficient inference** — speculative decoding and draft-token selection to improve LLM throughput and latency.

I am also building [**Reef**](https://www.reefinfra.ai/), open-source infrastructure for agents that learn from live interactions and continually improve their model weights and harnesses. Explore the project on [GitHub](https://github.com/Human-Agent-Society/reef).

I'm always excited to discuss research ideas, collaborate on projects, or simply chat about the fascinating world of AI and machine learning. Feel free to reach out via email, LinkedIn, or X!
